The innovation05 / 14

A process buffer is a battery whose state of charge is inventory — and its round-trip loss is derivable from electrochemistry, not assumed.

Data

IEX 15-min prices · MERC tariff order · plant historian (read-only)

Intelligence

Physics twin → flexibility cost curve φ(ΔMW,Δt) · LightGBM quantile price forecast

Decision engine

Stochastic MILP over 96 blocks, against landed cost, with CVaR₉₅ risk

Recommendation

Setpoint schedule + the binding constraint that produced it, in plain language

Operator action

Accept / reject per setpoint. Safety bounds sit outside the optimiser.

₹ value

Measured against the plant's own steady-state baseline

“The operating cell voltage varies almost linearly with the current density… Ucell = U₀ + k·i”

National Productivity Council (Govt. of India), Chlor-Alkali Sector Manual, p.28

Faraday's law makes production proportional to current, so power is quadratic and convex in production rate. Splitting output into a turn-down leg and a rebuild leg always burns more kWh than running flat. That excess is the physical cost of flexibility — the exact analogue of round-trip efficiency for a lithium cell.

Not claimed as new: buffer-as-battery, convex power curves, and chlor-alkali demand response are an established literature (Otashu & Baldea; Weigert et al.). New here: a four-year settled Indian price panel, a primary Indian tariff order opened to the page, and a landed-cost engine inside the optimisation.

Convexity fitted to NPC's own empirical curve (p.29) implies a quadratic coefficient of 0.30–0.45; we use 0.21 — our round-trip loss is the conservative one. published physics

Industrial feasibility10 / 14

Nothing in the plant changes. PRANA is a decision-support layer above the DCS, and the safety envelope stays outside the optimiser where it belongs.

What does NOT change
  • DCS / SCADA configuration — no write path
  • Interlocks, trip settings, relay protection
  • Product output — schedules are production-neutral by construction
  • Operator authority — every setpoint is accept/reject
  • Contract demand, connection, metering
What is required
  • Read-only historian export — 8 tags, 30 days (OPC-UA / PI / IP.21)
  • Rectifier kW, cell current & voltage, production rate, tank level, co-product consumer draw, billing kVA, outage log
  • One energy-manager owner; shift-in-charge briefing
  • A tariff copy and the open-access position
Failure modes, handled explicitly. Model wrong → advisory only at L1; the operator is the controller. Abnormal operation → a single-train outage is modelled as a derate, not a trip, and re-solves in 0.2 s. Infeasible → the system says infeasible and names the constraint; it never prints a rupee figure off a failed solve. Safety → the H₂-in-Cl₂ floor is a hard bound the optimiser cannot trade against.

The eight tags and the 30-day window are specified in ASSUMPTIONS.md A-14. Binding requirement is not elapsed time but ≥3 genuine load excursions in the window — a flat month teaches the twin nothing.
